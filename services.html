Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance? +
Yes, we recommend booking at least 24–48 hours in advance to secure your preferred time slot. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.
Are your products safe for sensitive skin? +
Absolutely! All our products are formulated with natural ingredients and are dermatologist-tested. Please inform our therapist of any allergies before your treatment.
How do I pay for a service? +
We accept cash, bank transfers, and card payments. A 30% deposit may be required to confirm bookings for premium packages.
Can I buy a service as a gift? +
Yes! We offer beautiful gift vouchers for all our services. Contact us on WhatsApp to purchase a gift voucher for your loved one.
What is your cancellation policy? +
We kindly ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a 20% fee of the service cost.
