SERVICES IMAGES
===============
Place your service card images here.

Required files (name them exactly like this):
  facial.jpg        → Facial Treatments card
  consultation.jpg  → Skin Consultation card
  scrub.jpg         → Body Scrub & Wrap card
  hair.jpg          → Hair Spa Treatment card
  manicure.jpg      → Manicure & Pedicure card
  massage.jpg       → Aromatherapy Massage card

Recommended size: 600 x 400px (landscape, 3:2 ratio)
Format: JPG or WebP

These are referenced in both index.html (preview cards)
and services.html (full service cards).

If no image is found, a coloured gradient background shows automatically.
