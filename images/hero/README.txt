HERO SECTION IMAGES
===================
Place your hero background images here.

Required files (name them exactly like this):
  hero-1.jpg   → Slide 1 background
  hero-2.jpg   → Slide 2 background
  hero-3.jpg   → Slide 3 background

Recommended size: 1400 x 800px or larger (landscape)
Format: JPG or WebP for best performance

These are referenced in index.html as:
  images/hero/hero-1.jpg
  images/hero/hero-2.jpg
  images/hero/hero-3.jpg

If no image is found, a green gradient background shows automatically.
